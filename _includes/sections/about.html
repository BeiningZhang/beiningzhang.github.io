About

I build trustworthy AI systems where mistakes are expensive: finance and health. The goal is not "AI adoption" — it is reliable decision support embedded in real workflows.

In finance, I build LLM systems that stay grounded under uncertainty: claim-first retrieval with citations for research and memo workflows, evaluation and monitoring loops that surface failures early, and workflow-first delivery with clear interfaces, rollback plans, and reliability targets.

In health, I work on high-agreement AI that respects expert time — including LLM-assisted classification and triage at scale, evidence-linked outputs with uncertainty handling, and human-centred systems aligned to clinical and research decision points.

My research interests include LLM-agent evaluation in mixed-motive and cooperative settings; governance, explainability, and participatory design for agentic systems; grounded generation; and quality monitoring through feedback loops, evaluation harnesses, and failure analysis.

Open to research collaborations on trustworthy AI, and speaking on LLM systems in high-stakes finance and health.
