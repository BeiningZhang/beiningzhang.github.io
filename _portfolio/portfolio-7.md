High-level overview of production work (no proprietary details):

- Designed distinct research strategies for local competitors, consolidators, and niche operators instead of forcing every buyer type through one scoring model.
- Turned an exploratory company-search workflow into staged, reusable research with explicit eligibility criteria, provider-purchase boundaries, enrichment, ranking, and durable company-data capture.
- Anchored candidates to canonical company identity and preserved provenance for reported, inferred, and calculated evidence so rankings remain reviewable.
- Defined a reproducibility framework for niche discovery using explicit market definitions, inclusion and exclusion rules, search paths, stopping conditions, Jaccard overlap, and rank-order comparison.
- Kept early delivery deliberately semi-automated, allowing adviser feedback to shape the workflow before committing to heavier infrastructure.

**Skills showcased:** Python, LLMs, web search, REST APIs, data modelling, entity resolution, canonical identity, evidence provenance, human-in-the-loop review, search evaluation, Jaccard similarity, rank stability, workflow design.
