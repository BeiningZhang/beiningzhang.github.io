High-level overview of production work (no proprietary details):

- Productionised exploratory buyer research as an asynchronous service with authoritative persistence, a documented internal integration contract, and an accumulated adviser-facing buyer universe.
- Expanded discovery coverage through region-specific research and composable industry filters with explicit semantics, while retaining per-method evidence and provenance.
- Kept product-fit and seller-preference signals separately attributable until the decision boundary, allowing optional context to improve ranking without hiding how a result was scored.
- Modelled incomplete execution as durable partial success with terminal timestamps, stage-level failure isolation, and targeted reruns so valid candidates survive identity or scoring failures.
- Preserved generated membership separately from adviser review state and reconciled canonical identity without erasing project relationships or forcing ambiguous matches.
