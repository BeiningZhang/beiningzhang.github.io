How can we imagine personal AI agents that understand human preferences without flattening the complexity of human life? Through the [AI Otherwise project](https://www.ccais.ac.uk/projects/aiotherwise/), I explored this question in the context of participatory workshops with everyday users and practitioners, examining alternative visions for trusted personal AI agents. The work connected speculative design, co-creation, and reflexive thematic analysis to identify what people value in future agents and what kinds of infrastructure may be needed for trustworthy deployment. It contributed to the paper [Three Lessons from Citizen-Centric Participatory AI Design](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&user=0Ze6XHYAAAAJ&citation_for_view=0Ze6XHYAAAAJ:2osOgNQ5qMEC), published in *Proceedings of Exploring the Participatory Turn in Citizen-Centred AI, an IUI '26 Workshop*.
